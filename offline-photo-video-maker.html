A photo video maker that works offline, with no account

StillsDrift makes the whole video on your phone. Your photos never leave it.

Nothing is uploaded

The video is made on your phone, so your photos stay private. The app doesn't need to send them anywhere.

Works offline

It works even in airplane mode. Only the links you tap, like the sign-up form or an email, open outside the app.

No account

Open the app and start. There's nothing to sign up for.

Free to use

The Free version includes every style and tool: Scrolling Columns, Photo Wall and Slideshow, in 9:16, 1:1 and 16:9. Videos use up to 3 photos at 720p and carry a small watermark. A Plus version with more photos is coming.
