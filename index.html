Questions

Good to know

Where do my videos go?

Tap Save. The video goes to your gallery, in an album called StillsDrift.

Which phones does it run on?

Android phones, through Google Play (testing now). An iPhone version is planned.

Can I remove the watermark?

The Free version adds a small StillsDrift mark. Plus is coming; leave your email and we'll tell you when it's ready.

Why does it say Beta?

StillsDrift is new and still growing. If something doesn't work as it should, tell us at support@stillsdrift.com and we'll fix it.

Something went wrong. What now?

Open the menu and send the log. It helps us fix it, and it doesn't include your photos.

Who made it

Made by professional photographers.

We're the photographers of REISS STUDIO. We shoot food, cars and fashion for brands, and we built StillsDrift for our own photos first.
