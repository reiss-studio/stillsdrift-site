Make a photo video for Reels, Stories and Shorts

StillsDrift turns a few photos into a short moving video, in the shape each place needs.

Pick the shape

  • 9:16 (tall): Reels, Stories and Shorts.
  • 1:1 (square): feed posts.
  • 16:9 (wide): widescreen video.

Pick the look

Scrolling Columns, Photo Wall or Slideshow. One set of photos gives you three different videos, so you can try each and keep the one you like.

Pick the pace

Calm, balanced or lively. A calm pace suits portraits and slow scenes; a lively pace suits busy sets of photos.

Keep the subject in the frame

Mark the main subject, like a face, a pet or a product, and every move keeps it in the frame.

Save and post

Tap Save. The video goes to your gallery, in an album called StillsDrift, ready to upload to any app.

StillsDrift is free, needs no account and works offline. It's in testing on Android now.
