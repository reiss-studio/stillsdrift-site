Support

Questions, problems or ideas: reiss.studio@gmail.com

Where do my videos go?

Tap Save. The video is saved to your gallery, in an album called StillsDrift.

What does the Free version include?

Every style and tool, every ready-made format, videos of up to 3 photos at up to 720p, with a small watermark. Face detection marks one face in each photo; you can paint more by hand. Plus, with more photos and more, is coming soon. Tell me when it's ready.

Something went wrong

Open the menu and choose to send the log. It goes to us by email or through the share sheet and helps us find the problem quickly. It doesn't include your photos.

Does it need the internet?

No. StillsDrift works offline. Only the links you tap (like the sign-up form or an email) open outside the app.

Deleting your data

Everything the app keeps is on your phone. Clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove it. To delete a sign-up or an email you sent us, write to us.
