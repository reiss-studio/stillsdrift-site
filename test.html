StillsDrift turns a few photos into a short moving video for Reels, Stories and Shorts. It's made on your phone, and your photos stay on it.

It's in testing on Android. Three steps, about two minutes. Use the same Google account in all three.

On iPhone? It's Android only for now. An iPhone version is planned: email us and we'll tell you when it's out.

  1. Join the testers group

    Being in the group is what gives your Google account access to the test version.

    Join the group

  2. Become a tester on Google Play

    Open the link and tap Become a tester. If it says the app isn't available, wait a few minutes after joining the group and try again.

    Become a tester

  3. Install from Google Play

    Install StillsDrift as usual. Updates arrive through the store.

    Open in Google Play

Please keep it installed

Google needs a group of testers who keep the app for a while before it can go on sale for everyone. Keeping StillsDrift installed, and opening it now and then, helps more than you think.

Tell us what you find

Something broke, felt slow or confusing? In the app, open the menu and choose to send the log. It goes to us sealed and doesn't include your photos. Ideas and questions: support@stillsdrift.com.

Doesn't work?

  • "Item not found" on Google Play: the phone uses a different Google account than the one in the group. Switch account in the Play Store, or join with the phone's account.
  • No Install button yet: joining can take a few minutes to reach Google Play. Close the store, wait, and open the link again.
  • An old version: open StillsDrift in the Play Store and tap Update.
