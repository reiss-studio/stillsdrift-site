How to turn photos into a video on your phone

StillsDrift is a free app that turns a few photos into a short moving video, made on your phone.

Three steps, under a minute

  1. Pick a few photos. A video plays right away.
  2. Mark what matters. Mark the main subject, like a face, a pet or a product. Every move keeps it in the frame.
  3. Choose a look and save. The video goes to your gallery, in an album called StillsDrift.

Three kinds of video from one set of photos

  • Scrolling Columns: an endless scroll of photos, in any direction.
  • Photo Wall: photos side by side, all moving.
  • Slideshow: one photo at a time.

You also choose the pace: calm, balanced or lively.

Good to know

  • It's free. The Free version includes every style and tool, up to 3 photos at 720p, with a small watermark.
  • No account. Open the app and start.
  • It works offline, even in airplane mode. Your photos are never uploaded.
  • It's in testing on Android. An iPhone version is planned.
